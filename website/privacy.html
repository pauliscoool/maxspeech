Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Summary

MaxSpeech is a desktop dictation app for Windows, macOS, and Linux. Live speech is transcribed through a speech API. Text may be cleaned with generative AI. We do not use your content to train MaxSpeech models. At most 10 recent session recordings may be kept on your PC for Remake — they are not uploaded for long-term storage.

1. What we collect

  • Account: email, username, user ID, and plan/settings synced to our MaxSpeech cloud database when you sign in.
  • Live dictation audio: streamed to our speech-to-text provider while you hold the hotkey so transcription can happen in real time.
  • Enhanced text: if AI enhancement is on, transcript text may be sent to an LLM provider to clean grammar and style.
  • Local history: dictation text is stored in a local database on your computer for the Home screen.
  • Local Remake cache: up to the 10 most recent successful dictation recordings as audio files under your MaxSpeech app data folder (for example under LocalAppData). Older recordings are deleted automatically.

2. Generative AI

MaxSpeech may use generative AI to improve punctuation, grammar, and tone. That processing is for delivering the feature to you. MaxSpeech does not use your audio or text to train our own models. Third-party providers (speech and LLM) process data under their own policies when requests are sent for transcription or enhancement.

3. What stays on your device

Remake recordings (max 10) stay in your local file directory and are not kept as a cloud archive by MaxSpeech. When you press Remake, that local file is sent again to the speech API to re-transcribe — the same kind of live API use as normal dictation — then the result can be typed into your focused app. Home history text can remain longer than 10 entries; Remake is only available when a local recording still exists.

4. Cloud sync

Settings and plan preference can sync to your account. Dictation history on Home is local by default. Cloud history sync, if enabled for your plan, is described in-product and remains limited to that feature.

5. Retention

Local Remake audio: newest 10 only. Account data: until you delete your account / log out and clear session as offered in Settings. You can delete individual Home entries anytime.

6. Security

We use industry-standard transport security for API and account traffic. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; use MaxSpeech on devices you trust.

7. Children

MaxSpeech is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The date above shows the latest revision.

9. Contact

Privacy questions: contact the publisher via the channels listed with your MaxSpeech download or account.
